Admission Requirements

Admission requirements for students applying with a British Curriculum certificate (IGCSE / GCSE / GCE A-Level).

General Conditions

  • 📋Must have completed Grade 12 or Year 13 with official documentation.
  • ⏳Early admission with predicted grades is allowed — final acceptance depends on official results meeting required thresholds.
  • 🏛️Certificate must be attested and equated by the UAE Ministry of Education.
  • 📊Students must meet the minimum percentage required for their intended program.

📖  The Grade 12 School Report (official final-year school report, not a British board certificate) may contribute an 8th or 9th subject. Grade 12 Arabic and Islamic Studies marks, like any additional board subject, are counted only when they increase the overall average.
